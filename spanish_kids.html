🔟 Decenas — Counting by tens

These take you all the way to 100.

🧩 Big numbers are like puzzles!

Once you know your tens and your 1–9, you can build almost any number by snapping two pieces together with y ("and"):

cuarenta+ y +cuatro= cuarenta y cuatro (44)
ochenta+ y +siete= ochenta y siete (87)
noventa+ y +nueve= noventa y nueve (99)

Tricky bit: 16–29 actually squish into one word instead — dieciséis (16), veintidós (22) — but 31 and up always use the two-piece "y" pattern.
